# Jinx-Projektilgrafiken

Erstellt am 23.10.2026 mit dem eingebauten Bildgenerierungswerkzeug gemäß der Imagegen-Skill. Eigene neu erzeugte Grafiken, keine extrahierten Originalsprites. Alle drei PNG-Dateien besitzen echte Transparenz. `tests/build_warehouse_assets.gd` erstellt daraus reproduzierbar `.res`-Texturen mit Mipmaps; das Laufzeitpaket enthält Bilder und Texturen. Die Kollisionsfläche bleibt unabhängig von transparentem Bildrand und Darstellungsgröße.

Dateien: `ultimate-rocket.png`, `small-rocket.png`, `rocket-explosion.png` in diesem Verzeichnis. Verwendung: `player_controller.gd` erzeugt die Raketen, `projectile.gd` zeichnet und detoniert sie, `runtime_effect.gd` animiert den Splash durch Wachstum und Ausblenden.

## Prompt: große R-Rakete

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished transparent 2D projectile sprite for top-down action videogame, single oversized ultimate rocket. Primary request: one massive chunky fantastical shark-nosed rocket flying horizontally toward RIGHT, centerline exactly horizontal; nose on right, engine nozzle on left. Thick squat cylindrical cobalt blue and gunmetal metal body, aggressive angular shark mouth painted near nose, vivid magenta fins, cyan energy strips, rivets and mechanical panels, white-hot orange exhaust at left. Hand-painted premium action game visual, crisp stylized realistic metal, camera directly overhead with subtle 3/4 top surface shading matching isometric/topdown arena. Composition entire rocket AND short exhaust fully contained centered on wide transparent canvas with ample clear padding all sides, isolated sprite, no ground or shadows behind. Make silhouette readable at 100 pixel wide gameplay size. No labels, no border, no text, no watermark, no background or checkerboard. ONLY one rocket, do not create explosion or multiple images.

## Prompt: kleine E-Rakete

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished transparent top-down videogame small rocket projectile sprite. Primary request: ONE compact slim small missile for a rapid salvo, nose pointing exactly RIGHT, engine LEFT, horizontal centerline. Premium hand-painted sci-fi game art, gunmetal and pale steel cylindrical body with cobalt blue panels, four small hot-pink stabilizer fins, luminous cyan stripe, sharp rounded white steel nose, short bright orange exhaust flame at left. Direct overhead camera with slight top surface shading, compact strong readable silhouette at 40-pixel gameplay width. Isolated single missile fully visible within wide transparent canvas with ample padding all sides. No scene, no ground, no text, no labels, no background, no checkerboard, no icon frame. This is the SMALL counterpart of a giant blue shark-shaped ultimate rocket, but avoid big shark face here: precise lightweight missile.

## Prompt: Explosions-Splash

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent VFX sprite for top-down action videogame, single rocket explosion impact. Primary request: a large explosive splash seen DIRECTLY FROM ABOVE, circular radial composition, concentrated white-hot yellow core, layered vivid orange flames and hot pink/cyan energy streaks with dark smoke lobes at the outer perimeter, sharp uneven starburst silhouette, scattered small glowing sparks. Premium hand painted game VFX with clean readable shapes at 200-pixel display size. All elements fully contained with generous transparent padding so edges cannot look cropped. Symmetric radial burst with some organic asymmetry, on a square transparent canvas. NO projectile, no ground, no environment, no text, no character, no labels, no black background, no checkerboard.
